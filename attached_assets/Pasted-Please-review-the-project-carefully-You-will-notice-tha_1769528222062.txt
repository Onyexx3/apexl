Please review the project carefully. You will notice that there is an admin feature for adding staff members, however the current staff creation form is incomplete.

The form does not include a password or role selection (Manager or Field Staff), which means that newly added staff members cannot log in to the system. This needs to be completed so that staff accounts are properly created with authentication and role-based access.

Additionally, please add a passport/profile photo upload field to the staff entry form so each staff member can upload their profile image.

Furthermore, the system currently does not support branch assignment. We need the following structure implemented:

Assign Managers to specific branches

Assign Staff to branches under a manager

Ensure that each Manager can only view and manage staff assigned to their own branch

In summary, the goal is to complete the staff management feature by enabling staff login, adding roles and profile photos, and implementing a proper branch–manager–staff hierarchy.
